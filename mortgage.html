What you borrow and what you pay back

Where each monthly payment goes

House price
what the home costs.
Down payment
money you pay up front from savings. In Canada it is at least 5%.
Mortgage (principal)
the loan from the bank for the rest.
Interest rate
the percent per year the bank charges for lending you money.
Amortization
how many years it takes to pay off the whole loan. 25 years is common.
Monthly payment
what you pay every month. Part is interest, part pays down the loan.
Total paid
all the monthly payments added up.
Total interest
total paid minus the mortgage. This is the cost of borrowing.

With less than 20% down, Canadian buyers also pay for mortgage insurance. It is left out here.

The monthly payment comes from a mortgage calculator. Canadian mortgages add interest twice a year and you pay every month.
