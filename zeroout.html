Zero Out Game

Get the total of your 8 cards as close to 0 as you can.

  1. Each player has 8 cards face down. Start by flipping any 2 of yours.
  2. On your turn, take the top card from the deck or the discard pile.
  3. Swap it for one of your cards. If it came from the deck, you can discard it and flip one of your cards instead.
  4. Opposites like +4 and −4 make a zero pair: they add to 0. Line one up in a column to see it glow.
  5. A Take away card removes one of your cards from your total. Taking away a negative makes your total bigger!
  6. When a player has every card face up, the other player gets one last turn.
  7. Your points are how far your total is from 0. Fewest points after the last round wins.
