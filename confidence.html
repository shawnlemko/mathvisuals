19 Times out of 20

The poll

“Do you want a 4-day school week?”

We can't ask every teen in Alberta, so each poll asks 1000 random teens.

Each poll gives a result ± 3. That makes a bar from 3 below to 3 above.

Did the bar catch the true %?

Polls: 0 of 20

In the newsA poll of 1000 Alberta teens found that 47% want a 4-day school week. The results are accurate within ±3 percentage points, 19 times out of 20.
