Mutually Exclusive Events

Mutually exclusive ✓ This one

The events can't happen at the same time. No overlap.

P(A and B) = 0, so P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)

Not mutually exclusive ✓ This one

The events can happen at the same time. They overlap.

P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B)
