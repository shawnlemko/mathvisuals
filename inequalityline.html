Inequalities on a Number Line

Draw this inequality

Closed dot: ≤ or ≥. The number is included.
Open dot: < or >. The number is not included.

How to draw

  • Tap the number line to put a dot.
  • Tap the dot again to make it open. A third tap takes it away.
  • Drag from a dot to draw the line. It stops at the next dot, or goes on forever.

Type the inequality

Correct: 0 of 0
